05

Method & limits

How it is computed, and what it cannot tell you

Where the numbers come from

  • US companies: annual 10-K, foreign issuers: 20-F, both read directly from SEC EDGAR XBRL.
  • European companies: ESEF annual reports from filings.xbrl.org.
  • Prices and FX: daily closes, free sources. Values are shown in the currency of the accounts.
  • Every week the pipeline cross-checks XBRL figures against a second source and publishes the gaps.

What is computed

  • Implied growth: the perpetual growth g that solves EV = FCF × (1+g) / (WACC − g). FCF is adjusted for stock-based pay.
  • EPV: value with zero growth, EBIT × (1 − t) / WACC − net debt.
  • Sustainable growth: ROIC × reinvestment rate, from the accounts only.
  • Lab: daily returns rebalanced to target weights; VaR and CVaR are historical (95 %, one day).

Limits

  • Missing data stays N/D: nothing is imputed.
  • A perpetuity formula is very sensitive near WACC = g; read the slope, not one point.
  • Past crises do not repeat in the same way. Holdings not listed at the time are excluded.
  • Rivals are an editorial pairing for one common market, not a full peer group.
Informational only, not investment advice. SAINHE publishes measurements and flags computed from public filings and market prices. It does not recommend buying or selling any security, and nothing on this page is a price target or a forecast.
